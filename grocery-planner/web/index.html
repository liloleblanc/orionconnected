Saw a price? Report it

Shelf prices you report count for two weeks for everyone shopping in your area. No photo needed.

Where the prices come from

  • This week's flyers for the grocers around your postal code.
  • Giant Tiger's regular shelf prices, from its public online listing.
  • Prices other shoppers reported nearby in the last two weeks.

Flyer prices are the store's advertised sale for the week; a regular price can differ between stores. Always check the shelf.

Prices are collected automatically and may be wrong or out of date.
