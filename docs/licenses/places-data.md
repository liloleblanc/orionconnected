# Places data: positions, regions, clocks and names

The map under the rows of the departures and arrivals boards ("Earth, Now",
`fids-current/js/fids-earth.js`) draws a route, a dot and a name for each
place on the page. Where those come from:

| Data | Where it is | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Airport positions (latitude, longitude) added in v24067 | `COORDS` (`fids-current/js/fids-core.js`), `AIRPORT_COORDS` (`fids-current/js/airport-coords.js`) | OurAirports, https://ourairports.com/data/ (`airports.csv`) | Public domain |
| Runway ends | `AIRPORT_RUNWAYS` (`fids-current/js/airport-runways.js`) | OurAirports (`runways.csv`) | Public domain |
| The US state or Canadian province of each code, and the list of Québec airports | `_ROW_REGION` (`fids-core.js`), `FR_FIRST` (`fids-current/js/board-strings.js`) | OurAirports (`airports.csv` `iso_region`, `regions.csv`) | Public domain |
| English city names added or corrected in v24067 | `CITY` (`fids-core.js`), mirrored in `fids-current/js/shared-names.js` | OurAirports: the `municipality`, without the county or region it adds after a comma and never the word Airport; where the municipality is a suburb, a district or a village, the city in the airport's own `name` (Rennes, Toulon, Jiaxing), or the city the airport serves when its name gives none (Palmerola: Tegucigalpa); for an island or island country one airport serves, the island's or country's name (Bonaire, Antigua, Dominica, St Kitts, Roatan, Faroe Islands, Mauritius, Jersey, Islay, Tiree, La Palma); where OurAirports joins two places ('Kokkola / Kruunupyy'), the one the airport is named for or stands in. The same rules were applied to the names already in the table (Turin for Caselle Torinese, Leipzig for Schkeuditz, Lille for Lesquin), except where the city's name is already another airport's (Williamtown, not Newcastle) | Public domain |
| French city names that differ from the English, added or corrected in v24067 | `CITY_FR` (`fids-core.js`) | Wikidata, the French label of the place the airport serves (https://www.wikidata.org/): Bakou, Mascate, Bergame, Cracovie, La Corogne … Where the English names the city, the French names the same city, never a suburb or two places joined | CC0 1.0 |
| Time zones | `ZONE_LIST`, `ZONE_ONE` (`fids-earth.js`) | The IANA tz database, https://www.iana.org/time-zones | Public domain |
| Positions of every other place the boards can meet, v24070 (every code in the board's tables and its country table, every code the live boards' rows were recorded with on 2026-10-19, and every airport with airline service and an IATA code) | `AT` (`fids-current/js/earth-places.js`, which only the map loads and reads) | OurAirports (`airports.csv`, read 2026-10-19; `scheduled_service` yes for the airports with airline service; the country table's codes also where it is no), three decimals | Public domain |
| Their clocks, v24070 | `ZONE` (`earth-places.js`) | The IANA tz database 2026e (`zone.tab`): the zone of the airport's country, and of its state, province or region (OurAirports' `iso_region`) where the country has more than one; the lines inside a state or province by the database's own comments. A place whose clock the database does not settle is left with none (`NONE`): the map prints no time there | Public domain |
| Names of those places that `CITY` and `CITY_FR` do not name, v24070 | `NAME` (`earth-places.js`): the map's own, read by nothing but the map; the rows, the gate, the companion app and every other screen keep `CITY`, `CITY_FR` and `shared-names.js` as they were | OurAirports, by the rules above: the `municipality` without a county or region after a comma, never the word Airport; the city in the airport's own name where the municipality is a district or carries a province code (Aosta, not 'Saint-Christophe (AO)'; Jask; Ndolo); the island's name where one airport serves it; written as the place writes it (N'Djamena, Mazar-i-Sharif) | Public domain |
| French names of those places, v24070 | `NAME_FR` (`earth-places.js`): none yet; a French board prints `NAME` | — | — |
| Where a name of those places is another place's too, v24073 | `IN_US`, `IN_CA`, `IN_ELSE` (`earth-places.js`): the state, province or country of the 69 places of `NAME` whose name `CITY` gives a town elsewhere, read without accents or St/Saint (YHG Charlottetown in Labrador, LOZ London in Kentucky, VLN Valencia in Venezuela, MRD Mérida in Venezuela, XLS Saint Louis in Senegal); the map prints it after the name, a country by its name in the board's language (the browser's `Intl.DisplayNames`) | OurAirports (`iso_region`, `iso_country`) | Public domain |

OurAirports dedicates its data to the public domain and asks for no credit;
it is named here and in `scripts/earth/README.md` all the same. Nothing on the
boards contacts any of these sites: the values are copied into the tables
once and checked by `tests/earth-floor-places.test.js`,
`tests/earth-zones.test.js` and `tests/earth-places.test.js`.
