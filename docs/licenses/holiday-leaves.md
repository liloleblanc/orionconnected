# Holiday leaves (Thanksgiving, Canada)

The maple leaves of the Thanksgiving holiday look ("A HOLIDAY LOOK: THANKSGIVING" in
`fids-current/css/display-overrides.css`, layers made by `_fidsHolidayLayers` in
`fids-current/js/fids-core.js`). The public tree carries no text files, so this note
lives here; each SVG also names its source in a comment on its first line.

## The files

| File (`fids-current/logos/holiday/thanksgiving/`) | Leaf | Used as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maple-gold.svg` | gold maple | the badge beside the board title on the silk banner; a small leaf on the gate ribbon |
| `maple-red.svg` | red maple | the gate band's main leaf |
| `maple-orange.svg` | orange maple | a small leaf on the gate ribbon |

## Source and licence

- **Source:** Vecteezy item 11215158, "autumnal leaves background" (a bouquet of maple
  leaves with swirls).
- **Licence:** Vecteezy Pro licence held for this project.
- **Changes made:** three single leaves cut out of the bouquet as vector data only —
  each leaf's own paths and gradients, nothing else; gradient stops thinned (the colour
  never moves by more than about 1/255), coordinates rounded to a tenth of a unit, and
  the canvas trimmed to the leaf. No change to shape or colour.

Vecteezy item 23481840, "vector of fallen leaves in autumn with color", held under the
same licence, was tried for this look and is not used: its leaves are all one orange-red
that sinks into the maple-red end of the banner, and their black offset shadow reads
heavy on the cream gate band.

Do not reuse these files outside this project without checking the licence terms.
