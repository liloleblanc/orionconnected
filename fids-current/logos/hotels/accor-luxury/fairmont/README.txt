Fairmont SVG logo pack from your supplied hotel list
====================================================

Included:
- 67 Fairmont hotel/property logos transcribed from the screenshot.
- Three colors: black (#000000), white (#FFFFFF), and gray (#808080).
- Outlined SVGs: the hotel-name line is converted to vector paths, so the files do not depend on fonts.
- Editable SVGs: the hotel-name line remains live text for quick future edits.
- PNG preview folders and contact sheets.

Folder guide:
- outlined_svg_black, outlined_svg_white, outlined_svg_gray: safest files for design/display use.
- editable_svg_black, editable_svg_white, editable_svg_gray: easiest files to tweak.
- preview_png_white_on_dark: white SVG previews shown on a dark background only for visibility; the SVGs themselves stay transparent.

Notes:
- The Fairmont wordmark is taken from the uploaded Banff Springs-style SVG reference.
- For hotel names beginning with “The Fairmont” or “Fairmont,” the lower line removes that repeated prefix so it matches the reference style, e.g. Fairmont + BANFF SPRINGS.
- “The Plaza” and “The Savoy” keep “THE” because that is part of the property identity in the screenshot.
