## Recommendations

1. **Major duplicate cleanup** — for AA, United, Air Canada you have 3–8 files that depict the same logo from different sources (freelogovectors, multiple revisions, etc.). Keeping one canonical per brand reduces confusion and saves CDN bandwidth. Tell me which to delete and I'll remove the engine references.

2. **Build out white/dark variants for regional carriers** — most US and Canadian regionals are color-only. For the "Operated by" badge on dark gate screens, a white wordmark variant would look cleaner than rendering the color logo on dark.

3. **European/Asian color base** — if any of these carriers ever need to appear on a non-row context (banner, gate header), they'll need a color SVG. Most don't have one. Low priority unless you operate flights to those destinations regularly.

4. **Perimeter file naming collision** — `Perimeter_Aviation_Logo.svg` and `perimeter-aviation-logo.svg` are both present. Engine uses the lowercase form. Delete one (recommend keeping the lowercase, deleting the capitalized).

### Important caveat

Auto-generated monochrome variants of multi-colour logos are SILHOUETTES
of the entire shape, not designer-curated single-colour treatments. They
look correct for most carriers (the trace is recognisable) but a few
gradient-heavy logos (Aeroplan, Spirit) may look flat or oddly blocky.
If any specific carrier needs a hand-cleaned version, the user can supply
a pre-made monochrome file and the auto-generated one will be overwritten.
